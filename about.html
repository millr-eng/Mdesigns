About M Designs

An independent service for clearly defined technical drawing work.

M Designs & Drafting is a small independent service drawing on Chartered Engineer status and practical experience in mechanical engineering, fabrication and construction project delivery.

The aim is straightforward: understand how a drawing will be used, work from agreed design information, and produce clear CAD files or models that help local trades and professionals deliver their projects.

Projects are taken on selectively, with a defined scope and realistic timescale agreed before work starts. The long-term ambition is to develop a broader engineering consultancy; the current offering is deliberately focused on drafting and modelling.

Background

  • Chartered Engineer (CEng)
  • Mechanical engineering and fabrication experience
  • 2D AutoCAD drafting and SketchUp modelling
  • Engineering and construction project delivery

CEng is a professional engineering qualification, not a claim that structural design, architectural certification or statutory approvals are included in the current service.

What matters on a small job?

Practicality

The drawing should communicate what the installer, fabricator or reviewer actually needs.

Clarity

Assumptions, scope, file formats and responsibilities are agreed up front.

Reliability

Jobs are only accepted where there is capacity to deliver against an agreed timescale.
