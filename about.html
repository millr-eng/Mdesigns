About M Designs

Practical, straightforward technical drawing support.

M Designs & Drafting is an independent service built around a background in mechanical engineering, fabrication and construction project delivery.

The approach is to understand how a drawing will be used, agree the required information and provide a useful deliverable without unnecessary complexity.

The service is operated on a project-by-project basis, with capacity and timescales agreed before accepting work.

Background

  • Chartered Engineer (CEng)
  • Mechanical engineering and fabrication experience
  • 2D AutoCAD drafting and SketchUp modelling
  • Engineering and construction project delivery

Qualifications and scope of professional services should be checked against current business arrangements before launch.

What matters on a small job?

Practicality

The drawing should communicate what the installer, fabricator or reviewer actually needs.

Clarity

Assumptions, scope, file formats and responsibilities are agreed up front.

Reliability

Jobs are only accepted where there is capacity to deliver against an agreed timescale.
