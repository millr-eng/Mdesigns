Quotation

Price based on complexity, references, number of outputs and revision requirements.

Quotes identify the agreed outputs, source information, assumptions, revision allowance and delivery period. Site visits, design changes, additional revisions and specialist engineering verification are excluded unless specifically agreed.

What affects a quote?

  • Whether the source information is complete
  • Drawing complexity and number of views
  • Required file formats and deliverables
  • Amount of checking and coordination
  • Number of revisions and timescale

What comes with an agreed scope?

A written description of the requested work, deliverables, price, assumptions, exclusions and agreed revision allowance. Any additional engineering verification is separately scoped.
