Frequently asked questions

Can you handle just one drawing?

Yes. Small, clearly defined drafting tasks are the focus of the service.

Can I send a handwritten sketch or a PDF?

Yes. The quotation will identify any measurements or design information needed before an accurate drawing can be produced.

Do you provide structural calculations or design certification?

Not as part of the standard drafting service. Any engineering design, checking or certification must be expressly agreed and is subject to competence, insurance and project requirements.

Can you work with clients outside Essex?

Yes. Where the required information can be supplied remotely, drawing work can be delivered to clients across the UK.
